## Method & honesty notes
- Sources searched 2026-11-19: web search (WhoTag influencer analyses for Egypt/Cairo, Nov–Dec 2025 snapshots), Mawthoq UGC marketplace (Egypt + Beauty verticals, live pages), creator portfolio sites, TikTok/YouTube public results.
- Follower counts are **aggregator snapshots (WhoTag, dated Nov–Dec 2025)** — treat as approximate and re-verify on the live profile before any deal.
- Two creator types are mixed deliberately:
  - **Type A — Micro influencers (10k–250k):** you buy content + their audience trust.
  - **Type B — Pure UGC creators (Mawthoq marketplace):** you buy the video file only (brand posts/runs it as ads). Follower count is not published/irrelevant — this is the cheapest, most controllable route for Zyra's 10 UGC concepts and matches the "real creators, diverse concepts" brief exactly.
- Market price context (KF Agency Egypt UGC/influencer pricing article, 2026): nano often barter/500–2,000 EGP per post; micro (10k–100k) roughly 10,000–42,000 EGP per post/reel in Cairo (beauty priced higher); a pure UGC video in Egypt typically starts from a few thousand EGP per video — far below influencer-post pricing.
