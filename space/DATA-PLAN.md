## Rotation-pointer repair (2026-11-19, surgical — rotation logic only)
- Live defect confirmed from production data: `runLoopTick` named «Era Care (ايرا كير)» in 12+ consecutive ticks (loop_runs ids 8–19) while `getOpsState` returned nextCompetitor = «Urban Ducks». Root causes: (1) two different coverage rules — tick used today-only + scope-only matching, getOpsState used all-time + scope+findings matching; (2) exact full-name substring matching — Era Care's round (id 9) was logged with the short scope «Era Care» (no «(ايرا كير)»), so the tick's `scope.includes(fullName)` never matched and the pointer froze, while getOpsState found the full name in that round's findings and moved on.
- Fix: one shared helper `nextCompetitorName(comps, rounds, now)` in server/src/actions.ts, called by BOTH actions. Rotation order = competitor id ASC (explicit ORDER BY). "Covered today" = a market round whose roundDate is today OR that was recorded today (Africa/Cairo business day) and whose scope+findings mention the competitor by full name, any separator-split name part (— – - ( ) ، , /), or a part's first word (≥4 chars). Once the due competitor's round is recorded, later ticks the same day advance to the next competitor and never re-name it; when every competitor is covered today, both return no next competitor. No design, typography, color, section, schema, or other data changes.
