# Winning Creatives Framework

## True Creative Diversity vs Variations

Andromeda retrieves on genuine diversity. Changing a background color, headline font, or first frame of the same concept is a **variation**, not diversity — near-duplicates trigger the **Similarity Penalty** (they compete with each other, inflate CPM, and split learning). A diverse set differs in at least two of: angle, hook mechanism, format, proof type, creator/persona, setting.

## The 6 Master Angles

Build the testing matrix across distinct angles, e.g.: Problem Agitation (name the problem in the customer's dialect), Ingredient Science (active + % as the headline), Authority/Expert (pharmacist/derm voice), Social Proof/UGC (real customer story), Comparison (us vs the alternative they already tried), Offer/Value (bundle math, risk reversal). Each angle x segment gets its own brief (Rule of One applies per ad).

## Winner Analysis — "Why It Won"

For every winner, decompose before scaling:

- **Hook layer** — what stopped the scroll (first 3s / first line, hook rate).
- **Hold layer** — what kept attention (hold rate, narrative structure).
- **Convert layer** — what closed (offer clarity, proof, CTA; conversion rate).
- **Economics layer** — CPP vs target, GPT/contribution verdict (brand financial rules decide, not CTR alone).
- Classify: **Validated** (economics + volume), **Promising** (signal, insufficient volume), **Declining** (fatigue curve bending). Track fatigue: CTR decay week over week; replace by the documented decay threshold, not by feel.

## Modular Remix

Winners are decomposed into reusable modules — winning hook, winning proof shot, winning offer frame ("wrapper"). Remix modules across new angles to produce the next test batch; log which module carried over so learning compounds.

## Libraries

- **Proven Wrapper Library** — validated hooks/wrappers with their evidence (dates, metrics, segment). Reuse before inventing.
- **Dead Angle Graveyard** — killed angles with the reason and data. Never retest a dead angle without a stated new hypothesis.

## Verdicts

KEEP · ITERATE · KILL · SCALE · INVESTIGATE · INSUFFICIENT DATA — assigned on 7-30 day windows against brand benchmarks, economics first (GPT/CPP), creative metrics second.
