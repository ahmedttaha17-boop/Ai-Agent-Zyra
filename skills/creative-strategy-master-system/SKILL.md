# Creative Strategy Master System

## ICP Psychology

- **5 Customer Needs** — map the need each product/angle serves before writing anything.
- **5 Whys** — drill from the surface want to the hidden emotional need ("why" x5). The 5th why is the ad's emotional core. If reviews/customer voice are missing, use analogous-product voice (same problem/skin type) and tag it ESTIMATED.
- **7Ws ICP segments** — Who, What (problem), Why (motive), When, Where, Which (product fit), What-next (retention path). Enforce the **3-to-7 Micro-Segments Scaling Rule**: fewer than 3 starves Andromeda retrieval; more than 7 fragments budget and data.

## Rule of One

Every ad carries exactly: **1 Emotional Need + 1 Resolved Objection + 1 Social Proof**. Two problems in one ad weakens both. One product URL per ad set.

## VCC Pillars

Classify every creative by job: **Visibility** (stop the scroll, pattern interrupt), **Credibility** (proof, authority, ingredient science, real results with honest timelines), **Conversion** (offer clarity, risk reversal, urgency that is true). A healthy account runs all three; diagnose gaps by pillar before producing more of the same.

## Written Creative Briefs

No vague notes. Every brief states: target micro-segment, need + objection + proof (Rule of One), angle and hook (first 3 seconds / first line), format and pillar, visual direction, copy skeleton, CTA, and the success metric + kill threshold it will be judged by.

## Offer & Pricing Engineering

Protect Contribution Margin first; lift AOV and delivery rate second:

- **Routine Bundles** — sell the complete problem-solving routine, not single items; anchor against the sum of singles.
- **Decoy Pricing** — a middle option engineered to make the hero routine the obvious value choice.
- **Free-Shipping Threshold** — set it **+15-20% above the hero AOV** so one add-on unlocks it; audit basket math for near-miss traps (e.g., hero + cheapest add-on landing 1-2 EGP below the threshold) and fix with a designed bundle.
- **Risk Reversal** — guarantee/exchange terms stated plainly at the decision point.
- **Pre-Payment Incentive** — 5-10% discount for InstaPay/card prepayment: raises delivered-rate economics (prepaid orders don't RTO like COD) and improves cash flow; model it against margin before offering.

## Scaling

- **Vertical** — more depth on a winning angle: new hooks, formats, creators, prompts modeled on the winner.
- **Horizontal** — new micro-segments and new Human Needs, launched monthly, each with its own briefs and test budget.
