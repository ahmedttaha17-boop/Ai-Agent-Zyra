# Meta Engineering, Domino & Creative Intelligence Frameworks

## 1. Meta Oct 2026 Engineering Pipeline
- **Andromeda 2.0 (Mixture of Experts Upfront Scoring)**: Evaluates ad candidates upfront at near-ranking accuracy based on User Experience and Customer Journey fit. Penalizes repetitive variations (`Creative Similarity Penalty`).
- **GEM (RecSys Foundation Model)**: Connects user engagement history over weeks (`Long-Context Learning`).
- **GenRecs (Cold Start Problem)**: New ads start at `Score = 0` and test on warm bottom-of-funnel traffic first before moving up-funnel to cold audiences. Never judge or kill a new ad in the first 48-72 hours (`Day Trade Trap`).

## 2. Charley T's Domino & 5 Olympic Rings (`3:2:2`)
- **One Hero Offer per CBO Campaign**: Consolidate budget into `1 Broad Ad Set` when weekly orders are `< 35/week` (`Tier 1`), `2 Ad Sets (1 Control + 1 Testing)` at `35-70/week` (`Tier 2`), and `3 Ad Sets` at `> 70/week` (`Tier 3`). Use `7-day click only` attribution and exclude `Audience Network`.
- **`3:2:2` Definition**: Strictly `3 Visual Assets (Proof / Story / Promise of the SAME format - never mix Video & Static) x 2 Primary Texts (2-line standalone hook+URL above See More + 150-200 word LLM Context Window below) x 2 Headlines (Identity Trigger + Offer/COD)` = **12 possible combinations**.
- **`5 Olympic Rings` Definition**: 5 distinct persuasion roles within a campaign (`3 Hello` upper-funnel roles + `2 Close` lower-funnel roles) serving 1 Hero Offer. They represent **different persuasion roles in the journey, NOT a mandatory sequential order every customer must see**. Theoretically `5 x 12 = 60` combinations, without requiring 60 live ads simultaneously.
- **`4PI+2` Status**: **`UNVERIFIED FRAMEWORK DEFINITION`**. Do not invent acronym expansions or scaling rules beyond explicitly verified account rules until an original primary source is provided.

## 3. Mostafa Qandil's Creative Diversity, CIS & Market Validation
- **Creative Diversity vs. Variations**: Changing 5 hooks on the same script is 1 angle with 5 variations, not 5 angles. Use the **6 Master Angles**: *Problem-Based, Benefit/Mechanism, Emotional, Social Proof, Objection-Smashing, Offer/Value*. Pass the **4-Question Similarity Audit** before launching.
- **7-Stage Proactive Flywheel**: `1. Research -> 2. Strategy -> 3. Written Brief -> 4. Production -> 5. 5-Question Creative Q&A -> 6. Testing & Analysis -> 7. Learning & Dual Scaling`.
- **5-Layer Creative Intelligence System (CIS)**: Tag every ad in English from the Brief: `ZYRA_[Product]_[Angle]_[Hook]_[Format]_[Talent]_[Lever]_V[#]`.
  - `Hook` -> `Hook Rate`
  - `Format & Talent` -> `Hold Rate & Outbound CTR`
  - `Angle & Lever` -> `CVR, CPP, AOV, Win Rate`
- **Modular Remix (`Win Rate vs. Spend Rate`)**: Pair the top Hook + beloved Talent from high-Spend ads with the high-converting Angle + Offer from high-Win-Rate ads.
- **Payday Cycle Hypothesis**: Treat as a `HYPOTHESIS` to verify against Zyra historical data (`Day 15-24` pre-payday dip vs. `Day 25-5` payday peak). Never kill validated campaigns solely due to mid-month dips without checking attribution and baseline.
- **3-Step Market Validation Triad & Lifecycle**: Validate via *(1) Google Keyword Planner/Trends*, *(2) Meta Ad Library/TikTok Creative Center (note: competitor ad duration is a supporting signal, NOT conclusive proof of profitability)*, *(3) Amazon/Noon/Jumia 1-3 star review mining*. Classify products as `Trendy`, `Seasonal` (launch `-30 days` before peak), or `Evergreen`.
