## History
- 2026-11-20: Ahmed sent the creative-research article and backlog template; both were studied the same morning.
- 2026-11-20: His research order was adopted as Zyra's: the ad account itself as a research database, then customers, then competitors and adjacent categories, then trends and formats. His backlog turns that research into a diversification map of live ads, an angles bank with evidence per angle, concept cards with a hypothesis, and testing with a decision rule written before launch.
- 2026-11-20: Six things from him were adopted into Zyra's working method: the five concept-priority filters (historical performance, business priority, awareness-stage fit, timing, production efficiency) as a ranking layer before briefs; a test card per concept stating in advance when to scale, adjust or stop; standard Hook Rate and Hold Rate formulas for video analysis after launch; creative production cost as a variable in deciding what to repeat; trend classification (fleeting moments, stable signals, deep forces) to guide the scout; and the diversification map applied to the Sunday launch set as a final audit.
- 2026-11-20: His method independently confirmed standing Zyra principles: real diversification is in the reason to buy, not the ad's shape; an angle without a documented source is a suggestion, not an insight; statics test direction first; and an ad running at a competitor is not proof it works.
- 2026-11-20: One difference was recorded: his list includes a 4:5 format, and Zyra stays on 1:1 and 9:16 by Ahmed's decision.
- 2026-11-20: Completing the series added a unified deconstruction language — one block per version, using the blocks hook, product, demo, problem, failed alternative, result, proof, story and CTA — with a one-block-per-version discipline after launch.
- 2026-11-20: Also adopted from the completed series: fatigue diagnosis (a steady Hook Rate with falling Hold Rate and CTR points to a body problem, not a hook problem); production-inclusive cost of result; prioritisation by impact x confidence x effort; prompt packs; a mandatory brief evidence field (a verbatim customer quote plus its source and date); a routing rule to fix the page, offer or tracking before making a new ad; value layers and five framings; and the note that an identity layer is missing for the bride and gym segments.
