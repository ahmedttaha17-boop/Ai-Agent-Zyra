## Zyra — Integrated Creative Operating Method (owner directive 2026-11-19 ~22:45)

Ahmed's standing order: everything learned (his rulings, Fraser Cottrell's process, the two format-list reels, the scout's directions bank, competitor pattern bank, search-language data) is merged into ONE way of thinking applied to every creative task — not separate files consulted occasionally. Goal in his words: strongest versions, biggest diversity and coverage, highest results and return. Every brief, analysis, or creative recommendation for Zyra runs this single stack, in order:

1. **Angle first (Rule of One):** one segment × one problem × one solution. The segment map (5 per hero routine + seasonal angles) and the problem come from recorded data — search language, customer voice, competitor gaps — never from invention.
2. **Format from the library, by its reference card:** the format is chosen from `creative/ad-formats-library-2026-11-19.md` and built exactly per its card in `creative/ad-formats-references-2026-11-19.md` (mechanics, composition, text, motion). A brief that doesn't cite its format card is incomplete. Visual direction comes from the directions bank (D0–D10) on top of the format, never instead of it.
3. **Fraser layer (always on):** half-second scrollability test — at phone size in a feed glance, problem + product must be identifiable or the piece is rejected pre-production; the hook does the targeting (call out the persona, show the persona, show her problem); message before imagery; every piece is a net-new idea, never a variation clone (Andromeda diversity is judged at composition level across the whole live set, not per piece).
4. **Video/UGC construction:** mirror casting (the creator IS the segment — look, dialect, environment); «5 أسباب» structure (each reason = routine step/USP + familiar scene + a real objection answered head-on; reason 5 = the uncopyable Zyra reason: complete 3-step routine + free shipping from the 2nd order + COD with pre-shipping confirmation); longer education pieces use the triple hook (audible + visual + on-screen text) and real b-roll only — no generic stock/AI footage; AI-UGC/EGC track follows the naturalness standard (short clips, hands/texture, real packshots composited, Ahmed's VO lane).
5. **Boundaries layer (non-negotiable):** banned claims and words, plain Egyptian language only, no shaming, retinol warning wherever the cellulite cream appears; the three bounded formats run only inside their written limits (formats library §7: Don't Buy This = real review only; Before/After = consented real photos, same light/angle, zero edits, true duration printed, supporting proof only, never TikTok, Meta fallback version always ready; Side Effects = honest-expectations framing only).
6. **Coverage check before any batch ships:** map the batch onto segments × formats × funnel stages × both routines. A batch that repeats a filled cell while an empty cell exists is sent back — diversity and coverage are checked as a matrix, not as a feeling.
7. **Measurement thinking:** statics/videos are diagnosed CTR-first (low CTR = message/hook problem; high CPM with good CTR = market/timing), then CPP against the standing gates (target 150–160, ceiling ~180, break-even ~275; peak-week judged vs ceiling/break-even, not target). Winners are re-angled into new formats from the library (winning message × new format), not cloned.
8. **The gate:** nothing leaves without the brief gate — one idea in one line, locked validator-passed copy, exact composition, verified product facts/prices, formats/fonts named, minimal on-design text, products big. Human designer executes final statics; Muse's visuals are labeled mockups only.
